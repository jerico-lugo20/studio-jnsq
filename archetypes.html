What this measures

Career equity, not personal branding.

Personal branding is how you present. Career equity is what you are actually worth to the market when you are not in the room: whether the right people already know your name, whether your credibility travels when your job title changes, and whether opportunity arrives without you chasing it.

Those are structural questions, and structures fail in a specific order. That is what the archetypes describe. Not a personality type, and not a score. The shape of what you have built, and the part of it that is not yet load-bearing.

The sixteen archetypes are read through the Market Authority Diamond™: Demand at the peak, Market Trust as the foundation, Credibility and Visibility as the two walls, and Branding as the centering point that holds them together. Branding sits at the centre because it is what everything else is organised around. It is not a facet.

The discipline behind it

Three things the market keeps confusing.

Branding is visual identity: logos, colours, messaging, taglines. The surface layer.

Brand architecture is portfolio structure: how a company organises multiple brands.

Brand equity architecture is the discipline of building the financial and reputational value of a company. It sits at the convergence of PR and Finance.

Career equity is that same asset class read at the scale of one person. Not a fourth thing, and not personal branding. The Market Authority Diamond™ measures the reputational half of it, which is the half that moves first.
