ROVIC Chicken Dealer

Batch Transaction Records — live-chicken dealing operation

A dealer buys chickens from provincial farms by the truckload, disposes them to talipapa sellers and eateries over a few days, then reconciles what's left. This tool models that exact rhythm: every truckload is a batch with its own P&L, receivables roll up across batches, and periodic audits prove the cash on hand matches what the books say it should be. All figures below are representative.

01 — DASHBOARD

The whole operation on one card wall

Six KPIs frame the business, then charts turn batches into a picture the owner can read in seconds.

  • ₱Full P&L chain. Revenue → CGS (capital) → expenses → net income, plus heads disposed and open A/R — the numbers a dealer lives by.
  • ▤Net income per batch. Bars go blue for profit, red for loss, so a bad truckload can't hide.
  • ◔Scoped views. All-time, by month, single batch, or a date range — the same wall, re-filtered.
  • ⚖Inventory reconciliation. Acquired vs disposed per batch flags "undisposed" or "unlisted/oversold".

02 — BATCHES

Every truckload, one row

03 — BATCH EDITOR

One truckload, fully accounted

04 — AUDIT RECORDS

Proving the cash is really there

Periodically the dealer counts everything they hold and checks it against what the books predict. This is the reconciliation engine — the feature that makes the whole system trustworthy.

  • ΣRoll-forward math. Prior ending balance + selected batch profits − non-op expenses = expected ending.
  • 🔒One audit per entry. Batches and non-op expenses lock to an audit so they can't be double-counted.
  • ⚖Liquidation breakdown. Cash, bank, inventory, A/R, floating & others sum to actual holdings.
  • ΔVariance, colour-coded. Expected vs actual surfaces shrinkage or an encoding slip immediately.

05 — NON-OP EXPENSES

The money that leaves outside a batch

Owner draws, household bills, loan interest — real outflows that aren't tied to any truckload. A fast journal that feeds the audit.

  • ＋Type-to-add row. The dashed bottom row promotes to a real entry the moment you type; a fresh blank appears. Tab keeps flowing.
  • ◫Groups. Entries cluster into named groups so a whole set can be pulled into an audit at once.
  • 🔒Audit lock chips. Once an entry is claimed by an audit it's read-only and tagged with that audit's date.
  • ⤓Bulk actions. Select to export, group, ungroup or delete.

06 — ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE

A running ledger per customer

Sales across every batch become debits; payments become credits. Each customer carries one running balance — proper double-entry, made legible.

  • DrDebit / credit tags. Sales (Dr) and payments (Cr) interleave chronologically with a running balance column.
  • ▸Expand on click. The header row is the balance; expand for the full ledger and drill back to the batch.
  • ↺Opening balances. Receivables that pre-date the system can be seeded so history stays whole.
  • ⚑Overpaid detection. Negative balances flag as "Overpaid" in amber, not hidden.

07 — HISTORY

Every sale line, searchable

Batches are the unit of work, but sometimes you need the flat truth: all sale lines across all batches, filterable and exportable.

  • ⌕Filter stack. Date range, customer, classification and free text combine to answer "what did they buy, and when?"
  • ↩Jump to source. A row click reopens the batch it came from — history is a lens, not a copy.
  • ΣLive totals. Qty, total, paid and balance foot the filtered set.
  • ⤓CSV export. The whole filtered view drops to a spreadsheet for the accountant.

08 — CUSTOMERS

Prices that fill themselves in

Each buyer has their own Good / Semi / Reject price. Storing them here means encoding a sale line is a name away from being priced.

  • ₱Default prices. Auto-fill into new sale lines — the encoder rarely types a price twice.
  • 🔒Locked accounts. "Donation" and "Retail" are system accounts that can't be deleted.
  • ⟲Version history. Snapshots auto-created before any destructive action; restore or download as JSON.
  • !Guarded danger zone. Reset-all sits behind confirmations and a fresh snapshot.
