What kind of businesses benefit from brand equity architecture?

Idiyanale Business Consultancy Services is an organizational transformation consultancy based in the Philippines. The firm partners with Filipino family-run businesses and medium-to-large enterprises navigating pivotal growth moments, from succession planning to regional expansion. Their track record includes engagements with conglomerates and large enterprises at the highest levels of business complexity.

Idiyanale's brand vision positions the firm as a trusted catalyst at pivotal moments for Filipino brands, bridging global excellence with profound, unique cultural wisdom. The 2026 strategy represents a deliberate market entry designed to make enterprise-grade capabilities accessible to a broader segment of Filipino businesses poised for regional and global competition.

Why do consultancies with strong expertise still struggle with visibility?

Going into 2026, Idiyanale had the expertise and the client outcomes but no market-facing infrastructure to match. Their presence was entirely word-of-mouth and referral-driven. There was no clear media strategy, no structured content system, no defined key messages, and no deliberate approach to how the brand showed up publicly.

The firm needed to launch a formal market presence. That meant building the entire foundation: positioning, messaging, content architecture, media readiness, and public-facing credibility, from scratch, within a defined timeline.

"We knew we needed to be more intentional about our media presence, but we didn't have a clear strategy or the internal capacity to build one from scratch."

— Frennie Hernandez-Moll, Managing Director

What does a Studio JNSQ media and PR strategy include?

Studio JNSQ delivered a 45-day Foundational Media and PR Strategy engagement structured across two tracks: Branding and Asset Development, and Media and Content Readiness.

  • Strategic positioning and messaging architecture, including brand vision, mission, and a 2026 market entry framework designed to position Idiyanale as descending from established credibility with major players, not as an emerging practice seeking validation.
  • Target market analysis and segmentation, mapping Idiyanale's primary audiences: family-run businesses at succession inflection points and medium-to-large enterprises pursuing regional expansion, with defined messaging for each segment.
  • A complete content banking strategy with a monthly production cycle, editorial calendar, content architecture across thought leadership, blog articles, and executive LinkedIn presence, and a defined production timeline with built-in review gates.
  • A media relations infrastructure including a full PR database, press release, media pitch materials, and an editorial planning system for identifying and pursuing placement opportunities.
  • A media training programme focused on narrative architecture and executive presence, preparing the founding team to communicate their positioning clearly and consistently across any engagement, including unexpected or ambush scenarios.
  • A visual branding system codifying the brand's identity across all public-facing touchpoints.

The entire engagement was anchored by the Market Authority Diamond™ framework, mapping Idiyanale's strategic objectives across Branding, Visibility, Credibility, Market Trust, and Demand to ensure every deliverable contributed to a compounding brand equity architecture.

"JNSQ didn't just hand over templates; the work felt tailored to where we actually are as an organization."

— Frennie Hernandez-Moll

How does a structured PR foundation change how a business operates?

Before the engagement, Idiyanale's market presence was reactive. After, it was architected.

The firm went from having no public-facing content strategy to a structured system they can sustain independently. They went from rebuilding credibility from scratch in every new conversation to having the architecture for a market-facing presence, including a website currently underway, built on the strategic foundation this engagement produced.

They are still in the early stages of building their public presence, but they are no longer building without a blueprint. The foundation is set. The direction is clear. The speed is there.

"Before this engagement, our market presence was largely word-of-mouth and referral-driven. Working with JNSQ gave us a framework, a clear message, a content system, and a media infrastructure we can sustain. The shift is from reactive to strategic."

— Frennie Hernandez-Moll
